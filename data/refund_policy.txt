Refund and Ticket Policy

All ticket purchases are final. Refunds are only issued if an event is cancelled by the organizer. If an event is cancelled, ticket holders receive a full refund to their original payment method within 10 business days. If an event is postponed, existing tickets remain valid for the new date, and holders may request a refund within 14 days of the postponement announcement.

Tickets are transferable. A ticket holder may transfer their ticket to another person for free through their account dashboard up to 2 hours before the event start time. Transferred tickets retain the same seat assignment.

Entry requires a valid QR code, which is emailed after purchase and also available in the account dashboard. Lost QR codes can be re-sent from the dashboard at any time. Doors open 60 minutes before the listed start time.
