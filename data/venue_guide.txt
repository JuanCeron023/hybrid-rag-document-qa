Venue Access and Accessibility Guide

The venue is fully wheelchair accessible. Accessible seating is available in every section and can be selected during checkout. Guests requiring an accessible companion seat should contact support at least 48 hours before the event.

Parking is available in the adjacent garage for a flat fee of 15 dollars per vehicle. The garage opens 90 minutes before doors and closes 60 minutes after the event ends. Electric vehicle charging stations are available on level 2 at no additional cost.

Prohibited items include outside food and beverages, professional cameras with detachable lenses, and bags larger than 12 by 12 by 6 inches. Small medical bags and diaper bags are always permitted. A coat check is available near the main entrance for 5 dollars per item.

The venue is a cashless facility. All purchases at concession stands and merchandise booths accept credit cards and mobile payments only.
